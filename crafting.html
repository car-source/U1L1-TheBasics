Minecraft Crafting Recipes

This page will include different crafting recipes for Minecraft, including the materials needed to craft each item and the steps to craft them. The recipes will be organized by category, such as tools, weapons, armor, and food.

Crafting Recipes

These are some of the basic crafting recipes you can use in Minecraft:

Tools and Weapons

  • Wooden Pickaxe: 2 sticks, 3 wood
  • Stone Pickaxe: 2 sticks, 3 cobblestone
  • Iron Pickaxe: 2 sticks, 3 iron ingots
  • Diamond Pickaxe: 2 sticks, 3 diamonds
  • Wooden Sword: 1 stick, 2 wood
  • Stone Sword: 1 stick, 2 cobblestone
  • Iron Sword: 1 stick, 2 iron ingots
  • Diamond Sword: 1 stick, 2 diamonds
  • Wooden Axe: 2 sticks, 3 wood
  • Stone Axe: 2 sticks, 3 cobblestone
  • Iron Axe: 2 sticks, 3 iron ingots
  • Diamond Axe: 2 sticks, 3 diamonds
  • Wooden Shovel: 2 sticks, 1 wood
  • Stone Shovel: 2 sticks, 1 cobblestone
  • Iron Shovel: 2 sticks, 1 iron ingot
  • Diamond Shovel: 2 sticks, 1 diamond
