Minecraft : A Sandbox Game made by Mojang Studios

Minecraft is a sandbox video game developed by Mojang Studios. Players explore a blocky, procedurally generated 3D world, discovering and mining ores, crafting tools and items, and building structures or finding structures. earthworks.

Why did I pick Minecraft?

I picked Minecraft because it allows for a ton of creativity and exploration as it is a sandbox game that is random with each world, enticing you to explore and find new biomes, structures, and resources. Additionally, you can play the game with friends, work on building projects together, and finding new resources and exploring new areas together.

What I already know

I already know the basics of Minecraft, such as how to build structures as bases, mine for resources, craft tools, and be able to survive on my own without any help from others.
